Meet the crew

Each bot does one job well, and they pass work to each other. Give Reception a video, a link or an idea, and the crew takes it from there.

Clipper

Finds the best 20 to 55 second moments in a long video or link, cuts them and reframes them for vertical 1080×1920.

Dreamer

Turns a one-line idea into an AI video: plans the scenes, generates the clips and joins them into one short.

Story writers

Seven genre writers, from sci-fi to kids’ rhymes, write original stories in your style, checked for originality.

Narrator

Makes long narrated, illustrated story videos with subtitles, chapters and a thumbnail, in a voice you choose or your own.

Editor and Captioner

Plans the cut with AI, burns in captions, adds hooks and sound effects, and checks every video before it is ready.

Scheduler and Poster

Books videos into your weekly posting times and posts through Upload-Post, Blotato, Ayrshare and others, or prepares them for you to upload.

Campaign finder

Searches paid clipping campaigns on Content Rewards, Vyro and Ssemble, checks every clip against the campaign rules and writes the captions with the right tags.

Influencer studio

Designs fictional, AI-labelled creators with a consistent look, then plans and makes their photos and short reels.

Watch your studio work

Bots walk to their desks when there is a job and show what they are doing in a speech bubble. On a break they head to the arcade, grab a drink in the kitchen or chat in the lounge. Errors pop up where you can see them, and a click clears them.

Name your studio and it appears on the big screen in the office.

With its own house band

A robot trio plays an original soundtrack on the lounge stage, cycling through swing jazz, bossa nova and a slow lounge ballad. The players move in time with every note you hear. Turn the sound on, or leave it off.

Questions

How do I get the app after paying?

Email us your payment reference and whether you have a Mac with an Apple chip, an Intel Mac or a Windows PC. Once we have checked the payment we email you the download link and the install guide.

Do I have to pay for AI as well?

Only for what you use, directly to the AI provider, on your own key. A free Groq key covers clipping, writing, captions and editing. AI video, voices and pictures need an xAI key, which is paid.

Is it a subscription?

No. The app is a one-time purchase with 12 months of updates. After that, updates are optional at £15 a year, and the app keeps working either way.

Are my videos uploaded anywhere?

HQ runs on your own computer. Files only leave it when a bot sends them to an AI service you set up, or when you post them.

Does it post for me automatically?

Yes, if you connect one of the supported posting services. Otherwise it prepares each video with a ready-to-paste caption for you to upload yourself.

Which Mac version do I need?

Open the Apple menu and choose About This Mac. If it says Apple M1 or newer, choose Apple Silicon; if it says Intel, choose Intel.

Will this make me money?

It saves you time making and posting content, and helps you find paid clipping campaigns. Earnings depend on your content and your audience, so none are promised.

Why does my computer warn me when installing?

The install guide shows the one-time step to allow it. The warning appears for apps that are not yet signed with an Apple or Microsoft developer certificate.
